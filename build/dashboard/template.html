About these numbers
PF / PA Index
Points for (or against) relative to that season's league average, so 1.00 is average and 1.10 is 10% above. It lets seasons with different scoring levels be compared.
Luck
Actual wins minus expected wins. Expected wins give each team credit each week for the share of other teams it outscored, as if it had played all of them. Regular season only.
Playoff odds
10,000 simulated seasons. Each remaining game draws both scores around the team's season average (pulled toward the league average early in the year) with the league's historical weekly spread. The top 6 by wins, then points for, make the playoffs. A simple model, not a forecast.
Win % (preview)
Same scoring model applied to a single game.
Streak arrow
How many spots a team moved in the standings versus the week before.
Records
Include every played game, regular season and playoff brackets, for all current and retired owners. Streaks carry across seasons.
Profit
Winnings minus buy-in, completed seasons only.
Data
League history sheet, 2009 to present. Game-level results start in 2011. Weekly lineups (Records pop-ups) start in 2019.
